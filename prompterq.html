PrompterQ is a single HTML file that runs in Chrome, fully offline. Mac or Windows, plus a second display or prompter monitor. Nothing to install.

For a live show with a real audience, yes. PrompterQ is the software our operators run. For a self-shot video or a rehearsal, you can run it yourself.
